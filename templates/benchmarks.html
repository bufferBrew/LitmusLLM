Benchmarks

Fixed question sets with known answers, scored by lm-evaluation-harness. This is the one measurement here that shares a scale with figures other people publish.

{# The distinction the whole app rests on, restated where someone is about to blur it. A benchmark percentage and a Litmus Score are not two views of one quantity, and the Reference page makes the same argument at length. #}

This is not the Litmus Score, and the two never mix

A Litmus Score is a judge model’s opinion of your prompts, on your scale. A benchmark score is accuracy against answers everyone can check, on everyone’s scale. Only the second can be read against a published figure — and even then, expect the same ballpark rather than the same decimal: prompt templates, answer extraction and shot counts all move a score by points.

The check worth doing first: benchmark a model whose published number you already know. If you land inside its interval, the harness is wired correctly. If you land eight points low, your setup is off — not the model.
